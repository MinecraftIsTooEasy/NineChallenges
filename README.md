# NineChallenges: SPD challenges

Adds 9 challenges inspired by Shattered Pixel Dungeon Challenges.

---

# Challenges

## Badder Bosses
`Bosses are much tougher with this challenge!`
- Ender Dragon:
    - +50% max health
    - +15% extra pillars in the End
    - Extra pillars and 25% of regular pillars have a second crystal embedded mid-pillar
    - Crystals stand on top of netherrack
- Wither:
    - +35% max health
    - Senses targets through walls
    - 15% of normal skulls become blue

## Hostile Champions
`You aren't the only who can level up.`
- Hostile champions spawn across the whole world
- One in ten creatures acquire one of six special champion types:
    - Blazing — +25% damage, attacks set victim on fire for 4 seconds, explodes in flames on death
    - Projecting — +25% damage, +50% reach
    - Antimagic — takes 35% less damage, can't be harmed if weapon involves magic
    - Giant — twice the size, +25% reach, takes 50% less damage, drops extra loot
    - Blessed — regenerates four times faster, can't be harmed without magic or silver aspects
    - Growing — grows stronger the longer it fights, starting with +20% damage and effective health up to +300%

## On Diet
`Food's already scarce, but you have to watch your portions as well!`
- Nutrition is thirded
- Satiation is halved, but never disappears
- Proteins and Phytonutrients are thirded
- If Nutrition leaves remainder after thirding, Satiation increases by 1
- Minimal nutrition is 5 half-shanks instead of 6
- Every 5 levels minimal nutrition increases by half a shank

## Faith is My Armor
`Take on the world with nothing but some leather to protect you.`
- All armor after leather is drastically worse, yet better armor still could be forged
- Protection enchantment is half as efficient
- Resistance effect is half as efficient
- Special protections are untouched

## Pharmacophobia
`Imagine how awful it is to be allergic to every illness cure. No need to imagine though.`
- Potions and consumables which gave Regeneration and Instant Health effects would now give Poison and Instant Damage effects respectively
- This change affects only player

## Barren Land
`There seems to be no healthy plants left in this accursed world...`
- The world looks sick
- Tree crowns are thin and small
- Leaves drop fruits only when mined with effective tool
- Saplings die
- 75% of flowers replaced by dead bushes
- Pumpkins barely spawn
- Cacti barely spawn
- Crops grow only on fertilized farmland, and every growth consumes manure
- Grass drops seed with 10% chance when mined by effective tool

## Swarm Intelligence
`Watch your back, they are going to follow you.`
- Monsters share location of their target to nearby monsters

## Into Darkness
`You can barely see past your nose.`
- True darkness just like in RemoteDarkness mod
- New moon's light is too dark to see anything
- Hell is in darkness
- In darkness hostiles spawn 12 times closer to player instead of 3 times closer
- Being in darkness slows player down by 20%

## Forbidden Runes
`What a great world of magic! Huh? You forgot to learn the language?!`
- Enchantment language is incomprehensible, as well as Enchanting Table
- Anvils could be used to work with magic
- It's still possible to carve runes on stones
